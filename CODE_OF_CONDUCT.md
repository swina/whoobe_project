# Code of conduct

Whoobe is an open project that wants small organisations, agencies, developers, architects, security people, writers and users from different fields to work
together. That only works if everybody can take part without being treated badly. This code of conduct applies to every space of the project: the
repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation and any place where someone represents the project.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive.

- Assume good faith and be kind to newcomers; remember that people have different experience, languages and time.
- Criticise code, ideas and decisions, not people. Be specific and explain why.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give credit.
- Say when something is outside your knowledge; ask rather than guess.
- Keep to the topic of the project, and respect the maintainer's time: it is limited.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or sustained disruption.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's origin, gender, identity, orientation, age, disability, appearance, religion or any similar trait.
- Sexualised language or attention, threats, stalking, or publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors, or abusing the reporting process.
- Spam, or using the project's spaces for unrelated promotion.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities of the maintainer

The maintainer clarifies the standard and may take fair, proportionate action in response to behaviour that breaks it: removing, editing or rejecting comments,
issues and contributions, warning, or temporarily or permanently banning someone from the project's spaces. The reasons are given to the person concerned
where it is safe to do so.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to **web@moodgiver.com** with the subject `Whoobe conduct`, or through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile
([@swina](https://github.com/swina)). Reports are handled with discretion, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent. If the concern is about the
maintainer, say so in the message and describe what you would like to happen.
