# For marketing and content owners

## What you can do yourself
Build and change pages and snippets visually; define forms and what happens when they are submitted (a record, an e-mail, a call to your CRM, a
flow in an automation engine) and show a different message by outcome; translate pages (AI draft, human review); manage images and files in the
media library; set SEO metadata per site; publish documentation; prepare a Brevo campaign from a page.

## What you will still need others for
The first connection to each system; designing flows in the automation engine; changes to the content model if you do not have the right to edit classes.

## What Whoobe will not give you
Personalisation by visitor, A/B tests, analytics, mass e-mail sending, ready-made connectors by the hundred. Keep your analytics, e-mail and CRM tools; Whoobe connects them
to your pages ([overview 4.4](../overview/whoobe-overview.md)).

## A good first project
One landing page with a form that creates the contact in your CRM and shows a message that depends on the answer.
